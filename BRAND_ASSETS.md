# Lityfied brand assets

This release preserves the exact brand language already used in the Lityfied project:

- **Get Lit. Get Satisfied. Get Lityfied.**
- **Find your vibe. Stay Lityfied.**

Included logo assets:

- `public/icon.svg` — recovered Lityfied monogram used by the application/PWA.
- `public/brand/lityfied-monogram.svg` — packaged copy of the monogram for brand reuse.
- `public/brand/lityfied-wordmark.svg` — platinum/purple/gold Lityfied wordmark lockup matching the storefront.

The visual system uses deep royal purple surfaces, platinum highlights, controlled gold accents, soft glass depth and restrained crown/royal details while preserving ecommerce readability and accessibility.
